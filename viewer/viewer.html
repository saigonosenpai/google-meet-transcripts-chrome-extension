    Sync

    Connect Downloads › Meeting Transcripts to bring back the history saved there (after a reinstall, or from another computer) and keep this library up to date with your other computers.

    1. Name this computer, then choose that folder. Chrome asks to let the extension read it: pick Allow on every visit.
    2. Using several computers? Put that folder in a cloud storage app installed on your computers (OneDrive, Google Drive for desktop, Dropbox or iCloud Drive), and connect it on each. How, for each app

    How it works: each computer saves its calls in that folder; your cloud storage app copies the folder between your computers; the extension reads the other computers' calls from it. The extension itself never connects to anything.
